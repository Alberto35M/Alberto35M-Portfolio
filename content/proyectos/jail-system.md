see every mob's and player's health at a glance

Add clean, Minecraft-style health bars above players and mobs, and know exactly how much health is left in any fight.


✨ Features
🎨 3 indicator styles: a detailed 20-state bar, a compact 4-heart bar, and a single minimalist heart.

👤 Players: name on top, health below.

🐷 Mobs: the indicator updates in real time.

⚡ Works out of the box: Bar 1 is enabled by default.

🔧 One command to switch styles.
