Lock Down Rule-Breakers in Style

Tired of troublemakers ruining your server? Put them behind particle bars, watch the countdown tick, and let them think about what they did.

Jail System adds a complete, polished punishment system to your Minecraft Bedrock world or server. One hit with the wand, or one command, and the player is locked in a glowing red cell with a live floating timer above their head. No loopholes, no escapes, no damage.

🪄 The JAIL WAND
Hit a player with the wand to jail them instantly. Set the duration and the number of uses, and the wand breaks when it runs out. Every wand is custom.

🛡️ Invulnerable Prisoners
Jailed players take no damage from anything, so your punishments stay fair and nobody dies in a cell.

🚫 Full Action Lockdown
Prisoners cannot break or place blocks, use items, or interact with blocks and entities. The cell is secure.

🧱 Protected Area
Everything within 4 blocks of a cell is protected from explosions, fire, block changes, and dangerous items like lava, water, flint and steel, end crystals, and ender pearls.

💾 Sentences That Stick
If a prisoner logs out, the clock keeps running and the cell is restored when they come back. No more “disconnect to escape”.

👑 Admin-Only and Safe
Only players with the admin tag can use it, and admins can never be jailed.


Commands
Command	What it does
/a:jail <player> <time>	Jail one or more players
/a:unjail <player>	Release jailed players
/a:jailitem <time> <uses>	Give yourself a custom jail wand
